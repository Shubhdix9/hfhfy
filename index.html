❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a fee for farmers to list crops or waste?

No. Listing agricultural produce and agro-waste is completely free for registered farmers.

How are machinery discounts applied?

Weekly rentals automatically apply a 15% discount, and monthly rentals apply a 25% discount calculated from the verified base daily rate.
